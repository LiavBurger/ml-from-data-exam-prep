Which format works for you?

The same material three ways: the gradient (regression note 5, plus the recap it needs) and the real exam part it prepares for, 2025-C Q1.2. Try each for a few minutes, then pick the one you kept going with.

A
One idea per screen Small cards you click through with Next/Back (or arrow keys), with a quick predict-then-reveal check every few cards, ending on the real exam part.
 B
Question first, teach just in time Starts with the exam question and solves it one line at a time; tap "why?" on a line only when you need the explanation.
 C
Short core, depth on demand Six lines, one formula and a three-line example on one page; each line opens the full explanation from the notes if you want it.

These pages are separate from the study site; nothing in it has changed.
